# Specification: PoC validation

Source: [PRD §§89 and 97](../../docs/Product%20Requirements%20Document%20v1.0.md#89-poc-definition).

- Exercise bounded work on Shipyard and an unrelated repository. Inspect the frozen contract, exact candidate SHA, deterministic checks, criterion decisions, and terminal evidence.
- Check that retained evidence remains intact and that incomplete criterion coverage prevents VERIFIED. Record gaps in observed scenarios and measurements.
- Record the owner decision on whether to proceed to the MVP.
